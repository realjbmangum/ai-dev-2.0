# The ceiling on autonomy

Decided 2026-09-06.

**Words always end at a human. Reversible data edits may auto-apply.**

## The line

- **Content is red, permanently.** Every X post, blog post, apparel concept, field guide
  and outreach email lands as a draft and waits. No graduation path is defined, because
  defining one now would be inventing a rule for a situation nobody has been in yet.
- **Reversible data edits are yellow.** An empty field filled from a checkable source,
  with before and after logged and one-click revert, auto-applies after
  `min_age_hours`. Tidy filling a missing `hours` value is the canonical case.

## Why the split is here and not somewhere else

The asymmetry is recoverability, not importance. A wrong data fill is one revert away
and the log says exactly what changed. A wrong post is on the internet, and a wrong
email to a real person cannot be unsent.

## What this makes the fleet

A very good writing and research assistant, not autonomous publishing. That is worth
saying plainly rather than discovering later, because it sets the honest expectation:
the fleet buys back the blank page and the research, not the decision.

## If this ever changes

It changes one verb at a time, on evidence, after months of drafts needing zero edits.
Not as a switch, and not on a tired evening.
